How it plays

  1. 1

    Watch it come

    The ball machine fires over the net. Its shadow on the court tells you where it will land.

  2. 2

    Swipe through it

    Your racket follows your thumb. Swipe through the ball when its ring turns gold, and the way you swipe is where it goes.

  3. 3

    Go for the winner

    Land it in the gold ring for a WINNER. Chain PERFECTs for up to 4x points. Let three by and it is game over.

NEW Doubles

Team up 2-on-2. You and your partner split your side of the court.

  • Your half, your ball. You hold the left or the right. A ball landing on your half is yours; your partner takes the rest, and the screen tells you when it is theirs.
  • Online or solo. Play doubles with people online, or with a bot partner against two bots.
  • Real doubles serving. The serve goes round all four players, diagonally across the net, like the real thing.
  • Never short a player. An empty seat is played by a bot, clearly labelled BOT, and if someone leaves mid-match a bot takes over.

NEW Play with friends

See who is online, invite them in real time, or text a friend straight into your match.

  • Who is online. The PLAY ONLINE screen lists everyone online right now, with an INVITE button next to each name. Your invite pops up on their screen.
  • Your own match. INVITE FRIENDS sets up a match with a 5-letter code that waits while your friends arrive. Start when you are ready; bots fill any open seats.
  • Text a link. TEXT A FRIEND opens Messages with a link to your match. They tap it and Rally Tap opens right into it. Pick them from your contacts on iPhone; your contacts never leave your phone.
  • Join with a code. Got a code from a friend? Tap JOIN WITH CODE and type it in.
  • Your name. Pick the name other players see. Block or report anyone with a tap.

Play online, 1-on-1

Tap QUICK MATCH and take on another player.

  • Real tennis scoring. 15, 30, 40, deuce, advantage. First to two games wins.
  • Serve and return. Swipe up to serve. The serve changes every game. Miss the ball and you lose the point.
  • Show off. Gold rings still pay bonus points, and the final scoreboard shows everyone's score.
  • Never wait. Nobody around? A practice bot, clearly labelled BOT, steps in.
