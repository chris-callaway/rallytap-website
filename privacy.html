Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Rally Tap is a game made by Gazellia Ventures LLC ("Gazellia", "we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: no account, no chat, nothing about you is stored on our servers. From version 1.3 the app tells Meta when it is opened so we can measure our ads, and online matches pass your shots to your opponent while you play.

What stays on your phone

Your best score and your automatic player name (for example "Player4821") are saved on your device. They are removed if you delete the app.

Online matches (version 1.3 and later)

When you tap PLAY ONLINE, the app connects to our match server at play.playrallytap.com. To run the match it sends your automatic player name and your game moves (shots, your position on court, points and score) to the server, which passes them straight to your opponent. Your opponent sees your player name and your moves, nothing else. There is no chat and no way to send messages, pictures or anything you type.

Like any internet connection, the server sees your IP address while you are connected. It uses it only to keep the match connected. The server does not save matches, names, moves or IP addresses. Its only records are anonymous counts, such as how many matches are running. When the match ends, it is gone. If nobody is available, you play a computer opponent labelled BOT and nothing is sent to anyone else.

Advertising measurement with Meta (version 1.3 and later)

We advertise Rally Tap on Facebook and Instagram. To know which ads work, the app includes Meta's software development kit (the Meta SDK). It tells Meta when the app is opened and when you finish a game. Meta also receives standard device information such as the device model, operating system, app version and IP address.

  • iPhone: the app asks your permission first, using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you tap "Ask App Not to Track", your device's advertising identifier is not shared and Meta is told not to use the data to track you. The game works the same either way. You can change your answer any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: Meta may receive your device's advertising ID. You can reset it or delete it any time in your phone's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

Meta handles this data under its own policy: Meta Privacy Policy. We use it only to measure and improve our ads. We do not sell your information, and we do not receive your name, email or contacts.

Earlier versions (1.0 and 1.1) do not include the Meta SDK or online play and send nothing anywhere.

Children

Rally Tap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete what we can.

Your choices

You can play without ever going online, decline tracking on iPhone, reset or delete your advertising ID on Android, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your phone. To ask about or delete anything Meta holds, use Meta's tools or contact us.

Changes

If this changes, we will update this page and the date above before any new version of the app does anything different.
