How it plays

  1. 1

    Watch it come

    The ball machine fires over the net. Its shadow on the court tells you where it will land.

  2. 2

    Swipe through it

    Your racket follows your thumb. Swipe through the ball when its ring turns gold, and the way you swipe is where it goes.

  3. 3

    Go for the winner

    Land it in the gold ring for a WINNER. Chain PERFECTs for up to 4x points. Let three by and it is game over.

NEW Play online

Tap PLAY ONLINE and take on another player, 1-on-1.

  • Real tennis scoring. 15, 30, 40, deuce, advantage. First to two games wins.
  • Serve and return. Swipe up to serve. The serve changes every game. Miss the ball and you lose the point.
  • Show off. Gold rings still pay bonus points, and the final scoreboard shows both scores.
  • Never wait. Nobody around? A practice bot, clearly labelled BOT, steps in.
  • Nothing to sign up for. You get an automatic player name. No account, no chat.
