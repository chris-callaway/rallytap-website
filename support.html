How do I play?

Balls fly over the net toward you. After a ball bounces, a ring closes around it and turns gold when it reaches the sweet spot. Swipe through the ball then for a PERFECT: your racket follows your thumb, and the direction you swipe is where the return goes. Taking it before the bounce is a VOLLEY. Anything else that you reach is GOOD. Your player runs to the ball for you. Let three balls past you and the game ends.

How does scoring work?

GOOD is 10 points, VOLLEY 15, PERFECT 20. A streak of PERFECTs and VOLLEYs multiplies every hit: x2 at 5, x3 at 10, x4 at 20. A miss resets the streak. Land a return in the gold WINNER ring on the far court for 30 bonus points, multiplied by your streak.

How does online play work?

Tap PLAY ONLINE on the title screen to be matched with another player. Matches are scored like real tennis (15, 30, 40, deuce, advantage) and the first to two games wins. Swipe up to serve. The serve changes every game, and whoever misses the ball loses the point. If nobody is available within a few seconds, you play a practice opponent labelled BOT. You need an internet connection to play someone else. Players only see an automatic name like Player4821, and there is no chat.

Can I reset my best score?

Deleting and reinstalling the app resets it.

Does the app collect data?

No account and no personal details. From version 1.3 the game tells Meta when it is opened and played, so we can measure our ads, and on iPhone it asks before using your advertising identifier. Online matches pass your moves to your opponent while you play, and our server saves nothing. See the Privacy Policy.
