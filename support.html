How do I play?

Balls fly over the net toward you. After a ball bounces, a ring closes around it and turns gold when it reaches the sweet spot. Tap the ball then for a PERFECT. Taking it before the bounce is a VOLLEY. Anything else that you reach is GOOD. Let three balls past you and the game ends.

How does scoring work?

GOOD is 10 points, VOLLEY 15, PERFECT 20. A streak of PERFECTs and VOLLEYs multiplies every hit: x2 at 5, x3 at 10, x4 at 20. A miss resets the streak.

Can I reset my best score?

Deleting and reinstalling the app resets it.
