Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

Rally Tap is a game made by Gazellia Ventures LLC ("Gazellia", "we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Rally Tap does not ask for an account, does not collect personal information, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your phone

Your best score is saved on your device so the game can show it to you. It never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the app.

Children

Rally Tap collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before any new version of the app does anything different.
