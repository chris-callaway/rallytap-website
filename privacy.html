Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026 (Rally Tap version 1.2 and later)

Rally Tap is a game made by Gazellia Ventures LLC ("Gazellia", "we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: no account, no personal details, and the only data that leaves your phone is what Meta needs to tell us which of our ads brought players to the game.

What we collect

Rally Tap does not ask for an account, your name, your email or your location. From version 1.2 the app includes Meta's App Events SDK, which sends Meta (Facebook) the following when you use the app:

  • That the app was installed and opened, with your device type, operating system version and app version.
  • When a game ends, your score and how many balls you returned.
  • Your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow it. On iPhone the app asks first ("Allow Rally Tap to track your activity?"); if you choose Ask App Not to Track, the identifier is never sent. On Android you can reset or delete it in your phone's Google settings.

What it is used for

Measuring and improving our ads on Facebook and Instagram: counting how many players an ad brought in and showing our ads to people likely to enjoy the game. We do not sell your information, and Rally Tap shows no ads inside the game. Meta's handling of this data is described in Meta's Privacy Policy.

What stays on your phone

Your best score is saved on your device so the game can show it to you. It is removed if you delete the app.

Your choices

On iPhone you can change tracking permission at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, Settings > Google > Ads. To ask us about any data related to you, email us below.

Children

Rally Tap is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this changes again, we will update this page and the date above before any new version of the app does anything different.
