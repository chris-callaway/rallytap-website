How it plays

  1. 1

    Watch it come

    The ball machine fires over the net. Its shadow on the court tells you where it will land.

  2. 2

    Wait for the ring

    After the bounce a ring closes on the ball and turns gold. That is your moment.

  3. 3

    Tap it back

    Nail it for a PERFECT, chain them for up to 4x points. Let three by and it is game over.
